# Glossary

**Broker**: a small, separate program that holds one dangerous power, such as accepting code or changing the cloud services, and uses it only when I approve, on its own page. Keeping each power in its own broker means breaking into one thing never gives you everything. (Chapter 8.)

**Context**: everything an AI can see when it answers: your message, the conversation so far, and any files, notes or instructions it has been given. It starts empty in each new chat, and it has a limit, so older details drop out of a long piece of work. (Chapter 4.)

**Prompt**: the message or instructions you give an AI. "Prompt engineering" is the craft of wording them just so. (Introduction, chapter 4.)

**Prompt injection**: an instruction hidden in text the AI reads, such as a web page, a note or a pull request's description, that tries to make it do something nobody asked for. (Chapter 8.)

**Pull request**: a proposed change to a project's code, waiting for someone to look at it and accept it. Accepting it is called merging. (Chapter 5.)

**Ring**: how private a part of the store is. Ring 0 is me alone; ring 1 is me and people I name; ring 2 is a project's people; ring 3 is the public. The store checks the ring on every request, whatever the AI has been told. (Chapters 4 and 8.)
